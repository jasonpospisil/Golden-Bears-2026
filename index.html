🏈 SEASON OPENER: It's the season opener, and you KNOW a celebration is in order!! Dust off your finest blue and gold, bring your biggest cheer, and come watch our boys take on St. James 6th!! Will there be a prize for the most Golden Bear gear? Only time will tell!!

  • ⏰ TIME CHANGE: Please note the game time has moved to 4:00 PM (arrive by 3:00 PM)!!
  • 🎪 Tailgate: How many Golden Bear families can we fit under one tent? Let's find out!!   • 🤝 Behavior: Let's set the standard for great sportsmanship!! Cheer loud and positive for our boys, respect the refs and our visiting families, and leave our space better than we found it. The last time St. James visited, it ended with a police escort for the refs to their cars (not our doing). I know our families have the utmost sportsmanship, so let's have that on full display!! Our coordinators and coaches are the best out there and will handle any situation that comes their way.
    • 📖 From page 9 of the league rules: "A player, coach, parent, or fan who is ejected from an athletic contest for any reason whatsoever must immediately leave the premises (including the field of play, stands, and adjacent areas such as nearby parking lots) and shall be suspended and unable to attend the next scheduled GCYL sponsored league athletic contest. If a coach, parent, or fan is ejected from an athletic contest for any reason, the youth player associated with that individual, without regard to whether or not the ejected individual is a parent of the associated player, cannot continue playing in the game and will be ejected from the athletic contest and must leave the premises. The associated youth player also may not participate in the next scheduled GCYL sponsored league athletic contest."

🗓️ FUTURE GAMES: Fair warning: our home game times might do a little shuffle throughout the season!! Fenwick is a happening place on the weekends (that campus stays busier than a beehive!!), and between all the activity and a brand new AD, our kickoff times may shimmy around a little. We're also working to get every team on the actual football field for every game. Consider it a fun way to keep us all on our toes!! Don't worry, I'll holler about any time changes the second I know, so keep those notifications ON!!

⏳ PLAYING TIME: According to the GCYL Game Rules and Guidelines, "the GCYL is a competitive football conference and there is absolutely no guarantee of playing time." Please know that some of our boys may not see much time on Saturdays, but every single one of them is getting valuable development three days a week at practice and on our Monday game days. PLAYERS should talk to their position coach first regarding any concerns or questions they have!! If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the Program Coordinator, Joe Toepfer. Every rep counts, and our coaches are invested in growing each and every player!!

📋 Organization Expectations

🏈 Player Uniform

★ Athletes should only wear team colors (blue and gold).
★ Athletes should always wear their Golden Bear team socks for games.
★ There is no restriction on cleats.
★ Don't forget mouth guards!
★ If a visor is used, it must be clear. No tinted visors.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 For Our Families

★ It is a requirement that every family volunteers for positions at our home games. A SignUp Genius will be coming out for signups.
★ Be model spectators on the sidelines. No foul language, no yelling at the refs, no coaching your athletes from the sidelines, and no negative interactions with visiting spectators.
★ Tailgating responsibility. Look after our shared space.
★ Leaving every space better than we found it.
★ No pets at games. Medical service animals are not included.
★ Being humble winners and losers.

🐻 The Golden Bear Way

🐻 We are a community first. Cheer loudly for our boys, support every family, and show up with good energy, on the field and off it. GO BEARS!! 💛💙🐻🏈🎉
