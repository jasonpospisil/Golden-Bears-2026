Jul 20, 2026 🐻 Welcome to the 2026 Season! ▼

Welcome back, Golden Bear families! We are SO excited to kick off another amazing season with you all!

📲 This page is going to be your go-to source for ALL things Golden Bears throughout the season!! You'll find weekly updates, the full schedule, important links, and so much more!! Bookmark it, share it, check it often, and add it right to your phone's home screen so it's always just one tap away, like an app!!

📱 iPhone (Safari): tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen."   Android (Chrome): tap the menu (⋮), then "Add to Home screen."

✅ IMPORTANT REMINDER: Please confirm your athlete's attendance with the Game Changer RSVP for EVERY practice and game!! This helps our coaches plan accordingly, so please keep it updated!! Thank you, Bear families!!

🎉 GOLDEN BEAR FEST is happening Saturday, August 22 from 10:00 AM to 12:30 PM!! Right after our Blue vs Gold scrimmage that morning, so make a day of it!! Our Parent & Coordinator Informational Session will be happening that same day!! Two for one!! Celebrate AND get all the season info in one stop!! Mark your calendars and we'll see you there!!

🎥 VOLUNTEER ASK: Videographer needed!! We are looking for someone to be our team videographer for the season!! If you've got a knack for capturing the action and want to help us preserve all the AMAZING memories our boys are about to make, please reach out to Coach Wietmarschen!! What a FUN way to be part of the team!!

👕 THE SPIRIT WEAR STORE IS UP!! Grab your Golden Bear gear and rep the team all season long!! Shop the store here!! Spirit wear orders will be delivered right to practice!!

🧦 GAME DAY SOCKS: Our team socks have been ordered!! They'll be delivered right to practice once they arrive AND once paid to Katie (see Venmo details in the Info section). These socks are required for game day!! They were already ordered at equipment fittings, so if you need a reminder of what was ordered, reach out to Katie!!

👖 OPTIONAL BELTS: IYKYK!! If you don't want your boys pulling up their pants as they run for a touchdown, these may be worth it!! Totally optional, but a handy little extra. Grab them on Amazon here!!

📸 ROSTER CARDS ARE COMING!! We're putting together roster cards so everyone can show off their player and follow along with the whole team on game day!! To make it happen, we need parents to submit photos of your athlete for their card. Examples will be provided (link coming soon!). Please get your photos in no later than July 27 so we can get these ready to GO!! Reach out to Katie if you have any questions about the photo!!

📋 Organization Expectations

🏈 Player Uniform

★ Athletes should only wear team colors (blue and gold).
★ Athletes should always wear their Golden Bear team socks for games.
★ There is no restriction on cleats.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 For Our Families

★ It is a requirement that every family volunteers for positions at our home games. A SignUp Genius will be coming out for signups.
★ Be model spectators on the sidelines. No foul language, no yelling at the refs, no coaching your athletes from the sidelines, and no negative interactions with visiting spectators.
★ Tailgating responsibility. Look after our shared space.
★ Leaving every space better than we found it.
★ No pets at games. Medical service animals are not included.
★ Being humble winners and losers.

🐻 The Golden Bear Way

🐻 We are a community first. Cheer loudly for our boys, support every family, and show up with good energy, on the field and off it. GO BEARS!! 💛💙🐻🏈🎉
